# ShopEase Demo Store - Return and Refund Policy

DOCUMENT TYPE: Synthetic Demo Policy
VERSION: 1.0

## 1. Return Eligibility
Customers can request a return within 7 calendar days after delivery.

Products must be unused and returned with their original packaging.

## 2. Non-Returnable Products
The following products are not eligible for return:
- Personal hygiene products after opening.
- Products damaged due to customer misuse.
- Items explicitly marked as non-returnable.

## 3. Refund Approval
Returned products are inspected after they reach the warehouse.

Refunds are initiated only after the return inspection is approved.

## 4. Refund Timeline
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider.

## 5. Return Request
Customers must provide their order ID and reason for return when contacting support.

## 6. Damaged or Incorrect Items
Customers should report damaged or incorrect items within 48 hours of delivery and provide supporting photographs.
