# ShopEase Demo Store - Cancellation Policy

DOCUMENT TYPE: Synthetic Demo Policy
VERSION: 1.0

## 1. Cancellation Before Dispatch
Customers may request cancellation before the order is dispatched.

## 2. Orders Already Dispatched
Orders that have already been dispatched cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.

Customers may contact support for further assistance.

## 3. Cancellation Refund
If cancellation is approved after successful payment, the refund is initiated within 5-7 business days.

## 4. Cancellation Request
Customers should provide:
- Order ID.
- Registered email address.
- Reason for cancellation.

## 5. Important Note
Cancellation approval depends on the actual order status.
