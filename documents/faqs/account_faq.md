# ShopEase Demo Store - Account FAQ

DOCUMENT TYPE: Synthetic Demo FAQ

## Q1. I forgot my password. What should I do?
Use the Forgot Password option on the login page.

Enter your registered email address and follow the password reset instructions.

## Q2. I am not receiving the password reset email.
Check your spam or junk folder.

Confirm that you entered the correct registered email address.

If the email is still missing, contact customer support.

## Q3. My account is locked.
Contact customer support with your registered email address.

Do not share your password or one-time password with anyone.

## Q4. Can I change my registered email?
Contact customer support and request an account email update.

Additional verification may be required.

## Q5. Is my password visible to support agents?
No. Customers should never share their passwords or one-time passwords with support agents.
