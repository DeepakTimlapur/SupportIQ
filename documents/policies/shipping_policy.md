# ShopEase Demo Store - Shipping Policy

DOCUMENT TYPE: Synthetic Demo Policy
VERSION: 1.0

## 1. Order Processing
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after successful payment.

Business days exclude Sundays and public holidays.

## 2. Delivery Timelines
- Standard delivery: 3-7 business days after dispatch.
- Express delivery: 1-3 business days after dispatch.

These are estimated timelines and may vary by delivery location.

## 3. Order Tracking
After dispatch, customers receive a tracking number through their registered email.

Customers can use the tracking number on the order tracking page.

## 4. Delayed Deliveries
If an order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, customers should contact support with their order ID.

## 5. Shipping Charges
Standard shipping is free for orders above Rs. 999.
Orders below Rs. 999 have a shipping charge of Rs. 49.
Express shipping costs Rs. 99.

These are fictional demo charges.
