# ShopEase Demo Store - Payment FAQ

DOCUMENT TYPE: Synthetic Demo FAQ

## Q1. What payment methods are supported?
The demo store supports UPI, debit cards, credit cards and net banking.

## Q2. What should I do if payment fails?
Check whether the amount was deducted from your bank account.

If the amount was not deducted, retry the payment after confirming your payment details.

If the amount was deducted but the order was not created, contact support with the transaction reference.

## Q3. What if I am charged twice?
Do not make another payment immediately.

Contact support with the order ID and both transaction references.

## Q4. How long does a failed-payment reversal take?
For this demo, allow 5-7 business days for the payment provider to process a reversal.

## Q5. Is cash on delivery available?
Cash on delivery is not supported in this fictional demo store.
